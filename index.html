About me

I got into coding after watching my brother write code. I loved the idea of bringing something to life, especially how animations work, and I haven’t stopped since. I enjoy building simple tools that help everyday people solve everyday problems, because that’s what the world really needs. My favorite project is PollyGlot. It started as a basic translator where you type a word and pick one of three languages. After a comment suggested I take it further, I turned it into a chatbot. It was pretty hard and I got stuck along the way, but I pushed through, and that’s why I’m proudest of it. Right now I’m focused on learning, growing my front-end skills, exploring AI engineering, and connecting with other developers. I’m open to an entry-level role or freelance work. Outside of code, I play competitive online video games, and who knows, maybe I’ll go pro one day.
